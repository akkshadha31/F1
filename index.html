TELEMETRY ARCHITECTURE & SCOPE

Transforming F1 Telemetry Into Championship Intelligence

In top-tier Formula 1 racing, the difference between pole position and midfield obscurity is measured in thousandths of a second. Millions of data points flow from onboard vehicle sensors, pit-wall telemetry streams, and race control feeds every Grand Prix weekend. Turning this avalanche of raw data into predictive racecraft requires advanced analytics and visual clarity.

Spearheaded and engineered by Akkshadha Rathore, the Formula 1 Race Intelligence Platform integrates 17 consecutive championship seasons (2010–2026), capturing 6,464 race outcomes across 29 international circuits. Powered by Tableau’s logical data relationship engine and an asynchronous Python Flask web service, this dashboard bridges raw telemetry with high-level championship decisions across 5 analytical pillars:

1. Global Race Footprint & Circuit Mapping

Geospatial telemetry projection spanning 29 Grand Prix circuits worldwide, analyzing historical race volume concentration led by Bahrain (2,168 logged outcomes), the United States (400), Monaco (328), and Spain (328).

2. Driver Performance & Pace Telemetry

Single-lap qualifying deltas, apex speeds, and race pace progression with dynamic Top-N scoring parameter controls (5–20 drivers) highlighting Hamilton (31,147 pts), Verstappen (31,126 pts), and Leclerc (27,673 pts).

3. Constructor Championship Supremacy

Longitudinal points trajectories and dominance cycles across multiple engine regulation shifts, tracking the epic constructor battle between Red Bull Racing (8,006 pts) and Mercedes-AMG (7,790 pts).

4. Pit Stop & Tyre Degradation Dynamics

Compound allocation analytics and stint longevity across Pirelli\'s 4 core specifications: Soft (31.52% of logged stint laps), Medium (30.78%), Hard (30.36%), and Intermediate (7.34%), quantifying tire degradation curves.

5. Reliability & DNF Diagnostics

Root-cause mechanical retirement analytics (Power Unit, Transmission, Brakes, Suspension) versus accident attrition, confirming modern hybrid engineering benchmarks with a 91.4% classified grid completion rate.
